Lectern 1.9.0

Lecture notes in Summary Studio, Process Lectures, smarter default models and automatic search by meaning

Changed

  • **Generate Notes is now Summary Studio's "Lecture notes" type**: overview, objectives, detailed notes by topic with slide citations and practice questions, key terms (into the subject glossary) and a summary, built from the same checked facts as every summary and checked against the slides. Notes from a link use it too.
  • **Process Lectures…** (Study menu, and a subject or folder's right-click menu) makes lecture notes, summaries, flashcards or Study Packs for every lecture in one place, replacing Summarise All…, Make Study Packs, Generate Flashcards for All Lectures and the Processing Queue menu item.
  • **Auto is the default model for each task**: your chosen model writes notes, summaries and chat; a small, fast model finds and checks facts and makes flashcards and quizzes (Haiku if you use Claude). Choices you made yourself are kept.
  • **Search by meaning turns on by itself** when an embedding model (such as embeddinggemma) is installed in Ollama; you can still turn it off.
