Lectern 1.5.3

Flashcards show which slide or page they came from

Added

  • New flashcards show which slide or page their information came from ("From Slide 7", or "Page 3 · Article" for notes from a link or file), checked against your notes. Click it to open that slide beside your notes, or the link or file. Shown when cards are created, in the page's flashcards panel, while studying, in decks and in the page's Flashcards section. Cards made from highlighted text get their slide too.
