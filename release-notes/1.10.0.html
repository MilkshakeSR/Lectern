Lectern 1.10.0

Hide-able writing toolbar, page timer with breaks, cleaner version display, and faster writing tools

Added

  • **Page timer.** A clock at the top of every page shows the time left while it runs (with a ring that empties, green on breaks, amber in the last minute). Its popover has Start/Pause, Reset, Skip, presets from 5 to 90 minutes or any time you type ("1h 15m"), a stopwatch, and breaks: short and long breaks with Classic 25/5, 50/10 and 90/20, auto-start, and progress dots. ⌥⌘P starts and pauses it from anywhere.
  • **When time is up:** a notification with Start break / Skip break / +5 minutes, a gentle chime (made by Lectern), a break suggestion, and optionally a dimmed page while you're on a break. If your Mac was asleep when it ended, you're told so when it wakes.
  • **Timer extras:** "What did you work on?" after each focus session (shown in Recent focus sessions on Home), a daily focus goal with a streak, focus sounds (rain, white noise, brown noise, café) and Quiet during focus, which holds Lectern's non-urgent notifications until the break.
  • **Outline** (⌥⌘O): the page's headings beside it; click to jump, drag to move a whole section.
  • **Find and replace** (⌘F, ⌥⌘F) with match case, whole words, highlights in the page and on the scrollbar, and one-step undo for Replace All.
  • **Writing helpers:** spelling in Australian English with your subjects' key terms never flagged, snippets (;def, ;ex…), key-term autocomplete (Tab), and a word goal per page.
  • **Open Beside:** two pages side by side (right-click a page, or Option-click it).
  • **About Lectern** shows the version, with the build number in a tooltip and Copy Version Details for bug reports.

Changed

  • **The formatting toolbar is hidden until you press the Aa button** at the top of a page (⌃⌘T, or View → Show Formatting Toolbar). It slides over the page without moving your text, and Settings → Editor can show it always or while you type. The selection menu, the / menu and every formatting shortcut work as before.
  • **One timer for the whole app.** The page clock, the menu bar timer and the Planner all control the same timer, which keeps exact time in the background, across sleep and after quitting. It replaces the old Pomodoro timer; your focus and break lengths carry over.
  • **The page header is one row of buttons**, with less-used ones in the … menu when the window is narrow. Choose and order them in Settings → Editor.
  • The version shows as "Version 1.10.0" everywhere (no build number in brackets).
  • New Settings tabs: Editor and Timer.

Fixed

  • Pressing ⌘Z straight after opening a page could bring back the previous page's text, which autosave then saved into the page you were on (BUG-018).
  • Summarising a page a second time could delete what you'd written below the summary when the page had no heading after it; Study Pack sections had the same problem for text typed after them (BUG-019).
