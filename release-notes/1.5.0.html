Lectern 1.5.0

See every file on a page, reorder pages, and click folders to open them

Added

  • A Files button (paperclip) at the top right of every page lists all the files and links added to it. Click one to open it, or show it in Finder.
  • Change the order of pages, lectures, quizzes, decks and folders: drag them, or use ⋯ → Change Order (Move Up, Move Down, Move to Top, Move to Bottom).

Changed

  • Clicking anywhere on a folder in the sidebar opens or closes it, not just the arrow.
  • New pages, lectures, quizzes and decks go to the bottom of their folder instead of being sorted A–Z. Your existing order is kept.
