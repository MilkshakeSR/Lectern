Lectern 1.3.0

Summarise files from your Mac, not just links

Added

  • “Notes from a link” now takes files from your Mac too: click Choose File… (several at once), drag files from Finder onto the bar, or paste a file's path. PDF, PowerPoint, Word, text and web-page files are summarised into notes at the bottom of the page, and a copy is kept in the subject's _attachments folder.
